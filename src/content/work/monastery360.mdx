## What it does

Monastery360 is a guided digital tour experience that layers map context, curated content, and audio narration around a heritage site. The goal is to make a place feel legible without requiring a physical guide at every stop.

## The constraint that shaped it

The main challenge was balancing storytelling and usability in a culturally sensitive environment. An experience like this has to feel immersive without becoming either overbearing or generic.

The experience is built as a narrative route: a location-aware sequence of stops, each with a short story and a map context. The whole model keeps the content legible and mobile-friendly without forcing the user into a heavy app structure.

## What I built

I designed and implemented the interactive presentation layer, including how the on-page path, navigation, and content blocks fit together into a cohesive virtual tour.

## What I'd change

I would tighten the editorial hierarchy and add more deliberate pacing around audio and map transitions so the experience feels less like a prototype and more like a finished guided visit.
