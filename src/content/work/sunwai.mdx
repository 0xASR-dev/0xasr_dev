## What it does

SUNWAI translates community demand into a clearer public-priority signal. The goal is not to replace decision-makers, but to make noisy asks legible enough to compare, defend, and act on.

## The constraint that shaped it

The hardest part was designing a system that still worked when the input was incomplete, messy, or contradictory. A public-systems tool has to make trade-offs legible without pretending the data itself is perfect.

The system ingests structured demand notes, weighs them against urgency and fairness, and surfaces a ranked budget signal instead of a raw pile of requests.

## What I built

I worked on the product logic, the data-flow design, and the dashboard narrative so it could explain trade-offs without feeling like a black box. The system was intentionally framed as a decision-support tool, not a claim that the merit of a project could be reduced to one number.

## What I'd change

I would add a stricter evidence layer so each recommendation links back to the original citizen theme, a policy assumption, and a documented risk or uncertainty.
